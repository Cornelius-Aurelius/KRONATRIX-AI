GEO vs SEO: different emphasis, shared foundations.

SEO focuses on helping useful pages become discoverable and competitive in search. GEO focuses on making information useful and understandable within generative search experiences. In practice, the work overlaps heavily.

Home / GEO vs SEO

The short answer

For Google specifically, current Search guidance treats generative-AI optimisation as part of SEO rather than as a separate replacement discipline. The practical reason to use “GEO” is to describe the extra attention given to generated answers, source selection, evidence and AI-search visibility.

Where they overlap

Useful content

Both depend on pages that genuinely solve the user's task.

Technical access

If the page cannot be discovered or indexed, generative-search visibility is constrained too.

Trust and evidence

Specific, supportable claims are stronger than vague marketing statements.

When should a business think about GEO?

After the normal search foundations are healthy. GEO becomes useful as a planning lens when customers increasingly ask full questions in AI-powered search and when your pages need clearer entities, direct answers, evidence or source-worthy material.

Do not create a second, duplicate version of every SEO page “for AI”. Improve the canonical page when the user task is the same.
