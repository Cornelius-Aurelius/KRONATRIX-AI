GEO vs SEO

SEO is the established foundation for search discovery and ranking. GEO is a newer label for improving usefulness and visibility as a source in generative search experiences.

AreaSEOGEO
Primary emphasisSearch discovery, ranking and useful search results.Source visibility and usefulness in generative answers.
Technical baseCrawlability, indexing, site structure and quality.Depends heavily on those same foundations.
ContentUseful pages matched to search intent.Clear, attributable information with strong evidence and context.
GuaranteesNo ranking guarantee.No citation or recommendation guarantee.

For Google Search, Google's current guidance explicitly says SEO best practices remain relevant to its generative AI features.
