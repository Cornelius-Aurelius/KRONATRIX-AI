What improves AI visibility?

There is no single switch. Strong visibility comes from combining technical access, clear entities, useful answers, original evidence, trustworthy public signals and measurement.

1. Be accessible

Important pages should be public, crawlable, internally linked and technically eligible for indexing.

2. Be understandable

Make the business, services, audiences, locations, authors and claims explicit enough to interpret correctly.

3. Be worth using

Publish information that adds real value: first-hand evidence, research, methodology, examples and useful answers.

Google and ChatGPT are not the same system.

Google generative search

Google says the same core SEO foundations remain relevant to AI Overviews and AI Mode. A page must be indexed and eligible to appear with a snippet before it can be used as a supporting link.

ChatGPT search

OpenAI says public websites can appear in ChatGPT search. Publishers that want summaries and snippets should not block OAI-SearchBot.

No ethical provider can guarantee that Google, ChatGPT, Gemini or another system will cite or recommend a particular page.

A useful AI visibility framework

AccessCan the system reach the page?
Entity clarityCan it understand who you are and what you do?
Answer qualityDoes the page solve the actual user task?
EvidenceAre important claims supported and specific?
CorroborationDo other trustworthy public sources support the same entity and facts?
MeasurementAre you tracking search visibility, referrals and qualified enquiries?
