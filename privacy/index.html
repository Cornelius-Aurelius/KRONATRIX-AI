Privacy notice

This page describes the main ways kronatrix.co.uk currently handles information submitted through the site.

Last updated: 18 August 2026.

Information you choose to send

When you contact KRONATRIX or use an enquiry form, you may provide information such as your name, email address, website URL and message. KRONATRIX uses that information to review and respond to your enquiry.

Forms and service providers

The enquiry form uses Formspree to receive submissions. Information submitted through that form is processed through that service as part of delivering the enquiry.

Analytics

The proposed launch version of kronatrix.co.uk does not load Google Analytics. If analytics or other tracking technologies are added later, this notice and the site's tracking controls should be updated before that change is made.

External websites

KRONATRIX links to external websites and specialist divisions. Their privacy practices may differ from this site.

Questions or requests

For privacy questions about information you have sent to KRONATRIX, email kronatrix.ai@gmail.com.

This is a practical site notice, not a substitute for professional legal advice. It should be reviewed if the site's data collection, advertising, cookies or service providers materially change.
