UK AI Search Access Index

A proposed repeatable research project for measuring the public technical signals that affect whether search and AI crawlers can access UK business websites.

What this page is — and is not

This page documents the proposed research design. It does not claim that KRONATRIX has already measured the UK market or produced a finished ranking.

The purpose is to make the methodology public before results are published, so future findings can be checked against a stated process rather than reverse-engineered after the fact.

Proposed methodology

1. Define the sample

Select a documented set of UK business websites using a repeatable sampling rule. The final study should publish the inclusion criteria, sample size, date collected and any exclusions.

2. Record public access signals

Inspect publicly available robots directives and other crawl-access signals without bypassing authentication, rate limits or technical restrictions.

3. Test normal search eligibility signals

Record whether important pages appear accessible, canonicalised sensibly and included in a discoverable sitemap where one is published.

4. Record AI crawler directives separately

Where a robots file explicitly names AI-related crawlers, record the directive as written. Do not assume that one crawler represents every AI product or use case.

5. Separate access from visibility

A crawler being allowed does not prove indexing, citation, recommendation or ranking. The index should report access signals without overstating what they cause.

6. Publish limitations

Document sampling limits, crawler-policy changes, technical ambiguity, unavailable files and the fact that external platforms can change behaviour over time.

Why publish the methodology first?

Reproducibility

Others can understand how the figures were produced and where judgement entered the process.

Trust

It prevents a marketing conclusion from being presented as if it were neutral measurement.

Future comparison

A stable method makes later editions more useful because changes can be compared over time.

Important: the scoring model, sample and first results are not yet published on this page. They should only be added after the data-collection method is finalised and the dataset exists.

Research should create evidence, not just more marketing copy.

This project is intended to become a citable KRONATRIX research asset only after the underlying data exists and the method is documented clearly.
