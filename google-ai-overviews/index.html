Google AI Overviews: what website owners actually need to know.

AI Overviews can summarise a topic or question and provide supporting links. Google says there are no additional technical requirements beyond the normal Search requirements for eligibility.

The eligibility foundation

Google's current guidance is straightforward: a page must be indexed and eligible to appear in Google Search with a snippet. Meeting the requirements does not guarantee indexing, selection or serving.

Indexed

The page has to be in Google's Search index.

Snippet eligible

The page must be eligible to appear in normal Search with a snippet.

Policy compliant

Normal Search technical requirements and spam policies still apply.

What Google recommends

Keep SEO foundations strongGoogle says existing SEO best practices remain relevant to generative AI features.
Create unique, useful contentOriginal, non-commodity information gives people a reason to use and cite the page.
Make important content textualCore information should not exist only inside an image or inaccessible interface.
Use internal linksImportant pages should be easy for users and crawlers to find.
Match schema to visible contentStructured data should accurately represent what users can see.
Provide a good page experienceMobile usability, clarity and speed still matter to visitors.

What you do not need for Google AI Overviews

No special AI schema

Google says there is no special schema.org markup required for AI Overviews or AI Mode.

No special AI text file

Google says files such as llms.txt do not improve Google Search visibility. They may still be maintained for other systems.

No tiny “AI chunks”

There is no requirement to break every page into artificially small blocks just for Google AI systems.

No page for every query variation

Creating large numbers of near-duplicate query pages is not a quality strategy and can cross into scaled-content abuse.

Measurement

Google introduced dedicated generative-AI performance reporting in Search Console in June 2026 and said it was initially rolling out to a subset of websites. Use the reporting when it is available for your property, while continuing to measure normal Search performance and enquiries.

Primary sources: Google Search Central's “AI features and your website” and “Optimizing your website for generative AI features on Google Search”.
