What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimisation is the process of improving content and website information so generative search systems can understand it and consider it when building an answer.

GEO does not replace useful content, technical SEO or a crawlable website. It adds stronger focus on clear entities, direct answers, evidence and information that can be interpreted accurately.

Full name Generative Engine Optimisation / Generative Engine Optimization.
Main purpose Make useful information easier to understand and evaluate in generative search experiences.
Replaces SEO? No. It relies heavily on established search and website foundations.
Guarantees citations? No. External systems control source selection and generated results.

Practical GEO foundations

Clear business identity

Make it obvious who the organisation is, what it does and how its pages and divisions relate.

Direct useful answers

Answer the real question early, then provide detail, evidence and limitations.

Original evidence

Publish data, case studies, methods, examples and first-party material that gives other systems something worth citing.

Technical access

Keep important pages crawlable, canonical, internally linked and present in accurate sitemaps.

Consistent public signals

Use consistent brand facts across the website and legitimate external profiles.

Measurement

Track real platform visibility and qualified enquiries instead of assuming a technical change caused an AI recommendation.
