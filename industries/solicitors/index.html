Industry guide

AI SEO for solicitors and law firms

Legal websites need especially clear service scope, solicitor identity, location, trust signals and careful answers to high-stakes customer questions.

What a solicitor website should make clear

Practice areas

Create clear pages for genuine areas of law rather than one generic services page.

Solicitor and team profiles

Show who provides the service, relevant experience and appropriate professional or regulatory information.

Location and jurisdiction

State offices, service areas and relevant scope accurately.

Client questions

Answer process, timing, costs or first-step questions carefully without turning marketing content into personal legal advice.

Evidence and trust

Use genuine credentials, public information, reviews or case evidence only where it can be supported.

Technical clarity

Keep important legal service pages crawlable, internally linked and consistent with visible structured data.
