Industry guide

AI SEO for estate agents

Estate-agent visibility depends on clear local expertise, genuine service coverage and useful information for sellers, buyers, landlords and tenants at different stages.

High-value website signals

Local expertise

Show real areas served, branches, neighbourhood knowledge and local evidence rather than cloned town pages.

Seller intent

Answer valuation, selling process, fees, timing and preparation questions clearly.

Landlord and lettings intent

Separate landlord, tenant and property-management information where services genuinely differ.

Real proof

Use verifiable listings, market evidence, reviews or case examples rather than fabricated success claims.

People and branches

Make the agency, team, offices and contact paths easy to understand.

Conversion

Give each intent a clear next step such as valuation, viewing, landlord enquiry or general contact.
