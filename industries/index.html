What stays consistent across industries

One real purpose per page

Do not build near-duplicate pages only to target another keyword or town.

Evidence before claims

Real credentials, reviews, sourceable facts and original evidence matter more than unsupported authority language.

Search intent before volume

Match the page to the customer's task: research, diagnose, compare, evaluate, buy, implement or troubleshoot.

Technical eligibility

Keep pages crawlable, internally linked, canonical, mobile-friendly and represented accurately in the sitemap and schema.

Start with the industry and customer problem, then choose the AI-search tactic.
