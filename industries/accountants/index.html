AI SEO for accountants

Make an accountancy website clearer about services, sectors, team identity, locations, proof and the questions prospective clients ask before making contact.

What an AI-readable accountant website should make clear

Services

Explain tax returns, payroll, VAT, bookkeeping, company accounts and specialist services accurately rather than hiding them behind vague labels.

Who you help

State relevant client types and sectors, such as small businesses, contractors or limited companies, only where they genuinely apply.

People and credentials

Show the real team, qualifications and trust signals that a prospective client can verify.

Location and availability

Make office locations and service areas clear without creating fake local pages.

Questions and process

Answer onboarding, timing, records, fees or next-step questions where the firm can do so accurately.

Technical structure

Use crawlable service pages, sensible internal links, accurate schema and clear enquiry routes.
