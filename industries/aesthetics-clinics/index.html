Industry guide

AI SEO for aesthetics clinics

Clinic websites need clear treatment information, practitioner identity, consultation and safety context, local relevance and honest expectations before a visitor is asked to book.

What the site should make easy to verify

Treatments

Use distinct, accurate pages for treatments actually offered and avoid unsupported medical or outcome claims.

Practitioner identity

Show who provides treatments and appropriate qualifications, roles or professional information.

Consultation and safety

Explain the real consultation process, suitability boundaries and important safety context with appropriate professional review.

Location

State the real clinic location and service area rather than creating fake local landing pages.

Proof

Use genuine reviews, images, credentials or evidence only when lawful and supportable.

Booking journey

Make the next step clear without creating misleading urgency or guaranteed-treatment expectations.

This page concerns website marketing and visibility. It is not medical advice or treatment guidance. Clinical claims should be reviewed by appropriately qualified professionals.
