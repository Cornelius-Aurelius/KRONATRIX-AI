What AI SEO means for an aesthetics clinic

The website should make the clinic, treatments, practitioner roles, location, consultation process, evidence and next steps easy to understand without publishing unsupported clinical or outcome claims.

Treatment pages

Give each substantial treatment its own clear explanation where appropriate, including what it is, who provides it, consultation process and important limitations.

Practitioner identity

Make real practitioner names, roles, qualifications and verifiable credentials easy to find.

Safety and suitability wording

Do not turn marketing pages into unreviewed medical advice. Important clinical claims and suitability wording should use appropriate professional review.

Local relevance

State the real clinic location and service area. Avoid thin pages for every nearby town.

Real proof

Use genuine reviews, practitioner information, consultation process and appropriately reviewed evidence. Do not invent ratings or outcomes.

Booking journey

Make consultation and booking steps clear, mobile-friendly and easy to complete.
