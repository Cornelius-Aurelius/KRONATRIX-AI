SEO vs GEO vs AEO: one website, three useful lenses.

SEO is the foundation. GEO adds a generative-source lens. AEO adds an answer-readiness lens. They should normally strengthen the same canonical pages rather than create three duplicate websites.

The KRONATRIX order of operations

1. Fix technical accessMake important pages crawlable, indexable, canonical and internally linked.
2. Match the real user taskCreate one strong page for each genuinely distinct need.
3. Make the answer clearUse AEO principles when a direct question deserves a direct answer.
4. Add source valueUse GEO thinking to add evidence, methodology, expertise and original information.
5. Measure outcomesTrack Search Console, analytics, AI referrals where available and qualified enquiries.

What not to do

Do not clone pages

A separate SEO, GEO and AEO version of the same answer usually creates overlap rather than value.

Do not chase every query variation

Google explicitly warns against creating large numbers of pages mainly to manipulate rankings or generated answers.

Do not promise citations

External systems decide what they index, select, cite or recommend.

Use the labels to diagnose the work.
