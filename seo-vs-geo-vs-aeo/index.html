SEO vs GEO vs AEO

Think of SEO as the search foundation, AEO as the direct-answer emphasis and GEO as the generative-source emphasis. Strong websites usually need the fundamentals behind all three.

TermMain emphasisPractical priority
SEODiscovery and performance in search.Technical eligibility, intent, usefulness, authority and page experience.
AEODirect-answer readiness.Clear answer, context, evidence and structure.
GEOGenerative source visibility.Entity clarity, evidence, usefulness and source trust.
Do not create three near-duplicate pages for every topic merely to target three acronyms. Create separate pages only when the user's task is meaningfully different.
