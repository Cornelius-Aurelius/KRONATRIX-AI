KRONATRIX / AI SEO Audit

What the audit reviews

1. Crawl and index signals

Robots rules, canonicals, sitemap coverage, internal crawl paths and obvious technical blockers.

2. Page purpose

Whether important pages have distinct jobs or overlap so heavily that they compete with each other.

3. Search intent

Whether each page answers the research, comparison, diagnostic or buying task it is targeting.

4. Entity clarity

Whether the business, services, divisions, people and relationships are described consistently and accurately.

5. Evidence and trust

Whether claims have credible support, first-party proof, sources, authorship and appropriate limitations.

6. Conversion path

Whether a qualified visitor can understand the offer and take the next step without confusion.

What the audit does not promise

KRONATRIX does not guarantee Google rankings, indexing, featured answers, AI citations or recommendations. Those results are controlled by external search and AI systems.

The purpose of an audit is to identify the signals you can actually improve and separate technical problems from content, architecture, evidence and conversion problems.

Typical outputs

Priority issue list

A clear order of what needs attention first rather than an undifferentiated list of hundreds of checks.

Architecture recommendations

Which pages should stay separate, which should be merged, and where stronger internal linking is needed.

Content recommendations

Where answers are too generic, unsupported, off-intent or duplicated across several URLs.

Technical recommendations

Where canonicals, crawlability, sitemap coverage, structured data or metadata need correction.
