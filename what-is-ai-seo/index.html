What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is the work of improving a website so its content, entities, evidence and technical foundations are easier to discover and understand across traditional and AI-powered search experiences.

AI SEO in plain English

AI SEO does not replace normal SEO. It builds on crawlability, useful content, clear site structure, relevance and authority, then adds stronger focus on direct answers, entity clarity, evidence and how information may be used in AI-generated search experiences.

Foundation Crawlable, indexable, useful web pages.
Content layer Clear answers that match the searcher's real intent.
Trust layer Accurate claims, evidence, authorship and consistent public signals.
AI layer Information that is easy to understand, extract and attribute correctly.
Guarantees No ethical provider can guarantee rankings, citations or AI recommendations.

How AI SEO, AEO and GEO fit together

SEO

Helps search systems crawl, understand and rank useful pages in search results.

AEO

Focuses on making content clear and useful when a system needs to give a direct answer.

GEO

Focuses on making content suitable for discovery, understanding and possible use in generative answers.

These areas overlap. The practical goal is not to chase an acronym; it is to make the business and its information easy for people and search systems to understand and trust.

What a strong AI SEO page needs

Clear intent

One page should solve one meaningful search task without competing with several near-duplicate pages.

Direct answers

Answer the main question early, then provide useful detail, context and limitations.

Entity clarity

State clearly who provides the information, what the business does, and how related pages and divisions connect.

Evidence

Use original data, real examples, credible sources and verifiable proof where the claim requires it.

Technical access

Use crawlable links, correct canonicals, sensible robots rules, mobile-friendly pages and accurate sitemaps.

Measurement

Track qualified enquiries, search visibility, indexed pages, branded demand and platform visibility rather than relying on promises.
