What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is the work of improving a website so its content, entities, evidence and technical foundations are easier to discover and understand across traditional and AI-powered search experiences.

AI SEO in plain English

AI SEO does not replace normal SEO. It builds on crawlability, useful content, clear site structure, relevance and authority, then adds stronger focus on direct answers, entity clarity, evidence and how information may be used in AI-generated search experiences.

FoundationCrawlable, indexable and useful web pages.
ContentClear answers that match the searcher's real intent.
TrustAccurate claims, evidence, authorship and consistent public signals.
AI layerInformation that is easy to interpret and attribute correctly.
GuaranteesNo ethical provider can guarantee rankings, citations or AI recommendations.

How SEO, AEO and GEO fit together

SEO

Helps search systems crawl, understand and rank useful pages.

AEO

Emphasises direct, clear answers that solve a specific question.

GEO

Emphasises visibility and source usefulness in generative search experiences.

The useful goal is not to chase an acronym. It is to make a business and its information easy for people and search systems to understand and trust.
