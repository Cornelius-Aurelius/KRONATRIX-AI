What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is the work of improving a website so its content, entities, evidence and technical foundations are easier to discover and understand across traditional and AI-powered search experiences.

AI SEO in plain English

AI SEO does not replace normal SEO. It builds on crawlability, useful content, clear site structure, relevance and authority, then puts extra emphasis on direct answers, entity clarity, original evidence and how information can be retrieved and attributed in AI-powered search.

FoundationCrawlable, indexable and useful web pages.
ContentClear answers that match the searcher's real intent.
TrustAccurate claims, evidence, authorship and consistent public signals.
AI layerInformation that is easy to interpret, retrieve and attribute correctly.
GuaranteesNo ethical provider can guarantee rankings, citations or AI recommendations.

What does AI SEO actually improve?

The useful way to think about AI SEO is to separate what a website owner can control from what a search or AI platform decides independently.

Technical eligibility

Make important pages accessible, indexable, canonical, internally linked and available in a clean XML sitemap. A page that cannot be accessed or indexed has fewer opportunities to appear in search.

Entity clarity

State the business name, services, audience, founder or accountable organisation, official profiles and important relationships consistently in visible text.

Answer quality

Answer the real question early, then support the answer with context, examples, limitations and next steps rather than burying the conclusion.

Evidence

Original research, real projects, transparent methodology, accurate sources and permissioned proof create information that is harder to replace with a generic summary.

Retrieval and navigation

Descriptive headings, crawlable links, focused pages and logical topic clusters make useful passages and related information easier to locate.

Conversion

Visibility is only useful if a visitor can understand the offer, verify the business and take a clear next action.

The useful goal is not to chase an acronym. It is to make a business and its information easy for people and search systems to find, understand, verify and use.

Does Google require special AI SEO?

No special AI-only markup is required for Google AI features. Google's current guidance says the same core SEO best practices remain relevant for generative AI features in Search. Google also emphasises useful, non-commodity content rather than special files or tricks.

That means the practical priorities remain familiar: make pages crawlable, indexable, helpful, well linked and supported by trustworthy evidence. Then improve the parts that matter particularly for AI-assisted retrieval: clear answers, entity relationships, original information and accurate attribution.

What about ChatGPT search?

OpenAI's current publisher guidance says public websites can appear in ChatGPT search and advises publishers who want content included in summaries and snippets not to block OAI-SearchBot. Allowing the crawler creates access; it does not guarantee that a page will be retrieved, cited or recommended.

OpenAI: Publishers and Developers FAQ

Search access

OAI-SearchBot is the crawler relevant to ChatGPT search discovery. Search access and model-training crawling are separate controls.

Useful source pages

Clear, specific and evidence-rich pages give retrieval systems a better source than vague marketing copy.

Measure referrals

Track referral traffic and qualified actions where data is available rather than assuming an AI citation created business value.

How to improve AI SEO: 7 practical steps

1. Fix access and indexingCheck status codes, robots rules, canonicals, sitemaps and accidental noindex directives.
2. Clarify the entityMake the business name, offer, audience, ownership and official profiles consistent.
3. Map real questionsUse Search Console, customer conversations and search research to find the questions people actually ask.
4. Give the answer earlyUse a direct answer near the top of the page, then explain the mechanism, evidence, limits and action.
5. Add original valuePublish real research, methods, examples, tools, datasets, project evidence or expert reasoning that generic pages cannot easily copy.
6. Strengthen internal linksConnect hub pages, definitions, comparisons, services, evidence and next steps with descriptive anchor text.
7. Measure and improveTrack indexing, impressions, clicks, AI referrals and qualified enquiries, then update pages from evidence.

Common AI SEO mistakes

Publishing lots of thin pages

More URLs do not automatically create authority. Similar pages can dilute focus and create maintenance and indexing problems.

Using schema as a shortcut

Structured data can improve machine understanding when it accurately matches visible content, but it does not guarantee ranking or AI inclusion.

Ignoring traditional SEO

AI-search visibility still depends heavily on the same foundations: access, indexing, usefulness, relevance, authority and good page experience.

Inventing proof

Fake reviews, fabricated statistics, invented locations or unsupported outcomes undermine trust and can create search, legal and reputation risk.

Optimising only for bots

The page still has to help a real person make progress. Machine-readable information should reinforce human clarity, not replace it.

Expecting instant indexing

Submitting a sitemap or requesting indexing is not the same as being indexed, ranked, cited or recommended.

AI SEO questions

Is AI SEO different from SEO?

It is better understood as an extension of SEO rather than a replacement. The underlying search foundations remain essential, while AI SEO adds extra attention to entity clarity, direct answers, evidence and retrieval across AI-powered interfaces.

Can AI SEO guarantee inclusion in AI Overviews?

No. A website can improve eligibility, usefulness and evidence, but Google controls when AI Overviews appear and which sources are used.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. GEO and SEO overlap heavily. See GEO vs SEO for the practical differences.

Should every business create separate AEO and GEO pages?

Only when each page has a distinct user purpose. Avoid near-duplicate pages created merely to target acronyms.

What should a business do first?

Start by checking technical access, indexability, entity clarity, content gaps, evidence and conversion paths. A visibility check or AI SEO audit can help prioritise those issues.
