Small business guide

AI SEO for small business

Small businesses rarely need dozens of AI-search pages. They need a clear website that explains the business, services, locations, proof and the real questions customers ask before buying.

Prioritise these foundations

Clear core pages

Home, service, about, contact and genuinely useful supporting pages should each have a distinct purpose.

Local relevance

State real locations and service areas clearly without mass-producing thin place pages.

Customer questions

Answer research, comparison, buying and troubleshooting questions where they genuinely help customers.

Proof

Use real reviews, examples, credentials, photos, data or case evidence where available.

Technical access

Keep pages fast, crawlable, internally linked, canonical and represented accurately in the sitemap.

Conversion

Give the right visitor a simple next step without pressure, fake scarcity or confusing forms.

AI SEO does not remove the need for normal SEO, reputation, useful services or good customer experience.
