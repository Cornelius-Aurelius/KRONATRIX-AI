What this framework does

The KRONATRIX AI Recommendation Readiness Framework v1.0 is a diagnostic model for reviewing the factors a business can improve before expecting strong visibility in search or AI-assisted discovery.

It is deliberately not presented as a ranking formula. No weighting is claimed to predict Google rankings, ChatGPT citations or AI recommendations. The purpose is to organise the work into clear areas that can be inspected, improved and measured.

Framework version 1.0 · reviewed 23 August 2026.

The five readiness areas

1. Technical accessImportant pages are public, crawlable, indexable where appropriate, canonical and connected through normal links.
2. Entity clarityThe business name, services, audiences, ownership, official profiles and relationships are consistent and easy to interpret.
3. Useful answersPages answer real questions at research, comparison, buying and troubleshooting stages with clear conclusions and context.
4. EvidenceImportant claims are supported with real examples, original data, sources, authorship, credentials or other appropriate proof.
5. Public corroborationRelevant third-party references, reputation and consistent external information support the identity and claims made on the owned website.
This is a readiness framework, not a guaranteed ranking formula. External search and AI systems choose their own sources, rankings and recommendations.

How to review each area

Technical access

Check status codes, robots controls, canonicals, XML sitemap coverage, internal links, rendering and whether important pages are actually eligible for indexing.

Entity clarity

Compare the website, structured data and official public profiles. The business should not appear to be several conflicting entities with different names, services or locations.

Useful answers

Map the questions customers ask before choosing a provider. Give direct answers, then explain conditions, evidence, limitations and the next useful action.

Evidence

Identify claims that need proof. Prefer original research, real project examples, transparent methods and reliable sources over unsupported superlatives.

Public corroboration

Check whether independent sources accurately support important business facts. Genuine reviews, professional profiles, press, directories and references can help when they are real and relevant.

Readiness states

Instead of pretending one score predicts an AI recommendation, KRONATRIX uses simple diagnostic states.

Blocked

A material issue prevents the information from being reliably accessed, indexed or understood.

Weak

The area exists but has clear gaps, contradictions, thin evidence or poor coverage.

Functional

The essential foundation is present and accurate, but there are meaningful opportunities to strengthen it.

Strong

The area is clear, well supported and useful, with no obvious material weakness found in the current review.

No state means a platform is required to cite, rank or recommend the business.

What recommendation readiness is not

Not an AI recommendation guarantee

External systems make their own decisions and can change their retrieval, ranking and answer-generation methods.

Not a replacement for SEO

Technical search eligibility, useful content, authority and good user experience remain foundational.

Not a schema score

Structured data is one machine-understanding tool. Accurate visible content and real evidence matter more than adding unsupported markup.

Not a volume contest

Publishing more pages or mentions does not automatically create more trust. Quality, relevance and consistency matter.
