What is Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO)?

AEO is the work of making information easy to understand, verify and use when a search or AI system needs to answer a specific question.

How AEO worksCheck your website

Answer the question first

Strong AEO content gives the useful answer early, then adds context, evidence, limitations and a clear next step. It works alongside SEO and AI SEO rather than replacing them.

A page should not become a collection of isolated one-line answers. The direct answer is the starting point; the surrounding explanation is what makes the answer accurate, useful and trustworthy.

How AEO works in practice

1. Match a real question

Start with a question customers actually ask, such as cost, suitability, requirements, process, comparison, timing or risk.

2. Give the direct answer

State the conclusion clearly near the top instead of hiding it behind a long introduction.

3. Add context

Explain when the answer applies, what changes it, important exceptions and what the reader should consider next.

4. Support the claim

Use real evidence, first-party examples, authoritative sources or transparent methodology when the answer depends on factual claims.

5. Connect related questions

Use descriptive internal links so the reader and crawler can move from the answer to definitions, comparisons, evidence and next steps.

6. Keep the page useful

Write for the person completing a task. AEO should improve clarity, not turn natural content into robotic fragments.

AEO example

Weak answer

“There are many factors to consider when choosing the right service.”

Stronger answer

“Choose the service that matches your actual problem, budget, evidence requirements and implementation capacity.” Then explain each factor, show examples, identify limitations and link to the next useful page.

The second version helps a reader immediately while still leaving room for the depth required to make the answer accurate.

AEO vs SEO vs GEO

SEO

Supports discovery, indexing, relevance, ranking and overall search performance.

AEO vs SEO

AEO

Emphasises answer clarity, context and usefulness for a specific question.

GEO

Emphasises source usefulness, evidence and visibility in generative search experiences.

GEO vs AEO

A good page can support all three at once. It can be technically sound for SEO, answer-first for AEO and evidence-rich enough to be a useful source in generative systems.

What AEO does not require

No magic word countUse the amount of detail needed to answer the task accurately.
No special AI-only schemaStructured data should accurately describe visible content; it is not a shortcut to AI inclusion.
No keyword stuffingUse the actual terminology naturally and keep the answer readable.
No fake FAQ expansionDo not manufacture dozens of low-value questions merely to create more indexed text.
No ranking guaranteeA clear answer can improve usefulness, but platforms control ranking and source selection.

AEO checklist for a business page

QuestionIs the page built around one clear user task or decision?
AnswerCan the main conclusion be understood within the opening section?
ContextDoes the page explain important conditions, exceptions and trade-offs?
EvidenceAre consequential claims supported by real evidence or reliable sources?
EntityIs it clear who is publishing the answer and what business or expert it represents?
NavigationAre related pages connected using descriptive internal links?
ActionDoes the reader know what to do next?

How AEO relates to Google AI search

Google's current guidance for generative AI features says established SEO best practices remain relevant. There is no special AEO markup required for Google AI Overviews or AI Mode. The useful AEO lesson is therefore about content quality and answer clarity, not a hidden technical trick.
