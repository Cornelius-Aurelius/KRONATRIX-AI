What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimisation is the work of making information easy to understand, verify and use when a system needs to answer a specific question.

Strong AEO content usually gives the useful answer first, then adds supporting detail, evidence, limitations and a clear next step. It works alongside SEO rather than replacing it.

Main purpose Clear, useful direct answers.
Best starting point Understand the exact question and search intent.
Replaces SEO? No.
Guarantees selection? No. Search and AI systems control their own results.

What makes a page answer-ready?

Lead with the answer

Do not make the reader search through a long introduction before getting the information they need.

Match the real intent

A research question, comparison question and buying question need different answers even when the topic is the same.

Add context

Explain conditions, exceptions and limitations so a short answer is not misleading.

Use evidence

Support factual or consequential claims with appropriate sources, data, examples or first-party proof.

Use clear structure

Descriptive headings, short sections, lists and tables can make complex information easier for people and machines to interpret.

Keep claims accurate

Do not add fake certainty, invented credentials or unsupported guarantees simply to sound authoritative.

AEO, SEO and GEO

ApproachPrimary emphasis
SEOSearch discovery, relevance, crawlability, technical quality and visibility in search results.
AEOClear, structured information that directly answers a user's question.
GEOClarity, evidence and source suitability in generative-search experiences.

In practice, the work overlaps. Useful websites need strong search foundations, strong answers and trustworthy information.
