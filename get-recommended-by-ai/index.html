Practical guide

How can a business improve its chance of being recommended by AI?

There is no guaranteed switch. Improve access, relevance, entity clarity, useful answers, evidence and genuine public corroboration so systems have better information to evaluate.

Build the foundations in order

Be accessible

Public pages need normal technical search foundations and should not accidentally block relevant crawlers.

Be specific

Clearly explain who you are, what you do, who you help and where your service applies.

Answer real intent

Give useful answers for research, problem-solving, comparison, recommendation and buying questions.

Prove important claims

Use genuine evidence rather than unsupported superlatives, fake reviews or fabricated case studies.

Earn corroboration

Build real references, reviews, links, mentions and reputation instead of manufacturing signals.

Measure outcomes

Track search visibility, AI referrals where measurable, branded demand and qualified enquiries.

No ethical provider can guarantee that ChatGPT, Google, Gemini, Perplexity or another system will recommend a specific business.

Start with the gaps you can actually inspect.
